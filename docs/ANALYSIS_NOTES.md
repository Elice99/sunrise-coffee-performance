## Data quality

Internal identities pass: four daily category columns sum to daily revenue in 100 percent of rows, net profit equals revenue less COGS, rent, staffing and redemption in 100 percent of rows, and the published ATV matches revenue divided by transactions within rounding in 100 percent of rows. There are no nulls in the checked columns and no orphan keys.

Known source problems: the brief says 31 December 2024 but the date table ends 30 December 2024. The dictionary says product margin is 36 to 80 percent, but the data runs 66.0 to 90.4 percent. `dim_date` has no `month_id`. The daily fact has an extra `avg_dwell_time_minutes` column. The workbook's `week_num` field holds day of week. 27 of 28 charts have no title. The Product, Loyalty and Store tabs have no cell values. Workbook status counts overlap, and one expansion KPI says 20 while the visible pivot totals 1.
